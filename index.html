◆ FAQs

Questions, answered.

Do I need prior experience to apply?+
No. Most of our tracks are designed for complete beginners. Some intermediate courses recommend basic familiarity, which we'll flag during admissions.
Are classes live or self-paced?+
Both. Every program includes live cohort sessions with mentors, plus self-paced material you can revisit anytime.
Will I get help finding a job after graduating?+
Yes. Our career team supports every graduate with CV reviews, mock interviews, and warm introductions to hiring partners.
Can companies partner with CodeBloom to hire graduates?+
Absolutely. We work with hiring partners across Africa's tech ecosystem — reach out through our Community page to get started.
Is mentorship available during the course?+
Yes, every student gets 1:1 sessions with mentors currently working at top African tech companies throughout the program.
